# ⏱️ Subtopic: Simple Harmonic Motion in Mechanics Guide

> [!ABSTRACT] Core Focus
> Differential equation $\ddot{x} = -\omega^2 x$, horizontal mass-spring, vertical elastic string motion, and Hooke's Law integration.

---

## 1. Vertical Elastic String SHM

At equilibrium: $m g = \frac{\lambda x_0}{l_0} \implies x_0 = \frac{m g l_0}{\lambda}$.
When displaced by $x$ below equilibrium:
$$m \ddot{x} = m g - \frac{\lambda (x_0 + x)}{l_0} = -\left(\frac{\lambda}{l_0}\right) x \implies \ddot{x} = -\left(\frac{\lambda}{m l_0}\right) x$$
This is SHM with $\omega^2 = \frac{\lambda}{m l_0}$ and period $T = 2\pi \sqrt{\frac{m l_0}{\lambda}}$.
