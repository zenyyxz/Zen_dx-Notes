## 1. Tangency Condition ($p = r$)

Line $Ax + By + C = 0$ is tangent to circle $(x - h)^2 + (y - k)^2 = r^2$ if and only if:
$$\frac{|A h + B k + C|}{\sqrt{A^2 + B^2}} = r$$
