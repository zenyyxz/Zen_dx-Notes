## 1. Roots of Unity

Solutions to $z^n = 1$:
$$z_k = e^{i \frac{2k\pi}{n}} = \cos\left(\frac{2k\pi}{n}\right) + i \sin\left(\frac{2k\pi}{n}\right), \quad k = 0, 1, \dots, n-1$$
- Sum of all $n$-th roots of unity is $0$.
- The roots form the vertices of a regular $n$-sided polygon centered at origin on unit circle.
