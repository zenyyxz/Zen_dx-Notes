# :LiArrowBigUp: Subtopic: Differentiation & Curve Sketching Guide

---
## 1. 7-Step Curve Sketching Algorithm

1. Domain & Symmetry (even/odd).
2. Intercepts with axes ($x=0$ and $y=0$).
3. Vertical Asymptotes (denom $= 0$) & Horizontal/Oblique Asymptotes ($\lim_{x \to \pm\infty}$).
4. First Derivative $f'(x)$ & Stationary points ($f'(x) = 0$).
5. Intervals of increase/decrease.
6. Second Derivative $f''(x)$, Concavity & Points of Inflection.
7. Plot points and sketch smooth curve.
