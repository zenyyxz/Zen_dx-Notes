## 1. Half-Angle $t = \tan(\theta/2)$ Substitution

$$\sin\theta = \frac{2t}{1+t^2}, \quad \cos\theta = \frac{1-t^2}{1+t^2}, \quad \tan\theta = \frac{2t}{1-t^2}$$
Used to convert trigonometric rational equations into algebraic polynomial equations in $t$.
