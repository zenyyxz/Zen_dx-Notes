## 1. E-Business vs. E-Commerce

- **E-Business (Electronic Business)**: The transformation of key business processes through the use of internet technologies (includes internal management, ERP, SCM, CRM, and E-Commerce).
- **E-Commerce (Electronic Commerce)**: A subset of E-Business specifically referring to buying and selling goods and services online over the internet.

---
## 2. E-Commerce Business Models

| Model | Full Name | Description | Example |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **B2B** | Business-to-Business | Transactions between two businesses (wholesalers/manufacturers). | Alibaba, Boeing portal |
| **B2C** | Business-to-Consumer | Online retail store selling directly to individual consumers. | Amazon, Daraz |
| **C2C** | Consumer-to-Consumer | Individuals selling directly to other individuals online. | eBay, ikman.lk |
| **C2B** | Consumer-to-Business | Individuals offering products/services to businesses. | Upwork, Fiverr freelancers |
| **G2C** | Government-to-Citizen | Government offering digital e-services to public citizens. | E-Revenue portal |

---
## 3. E-Payment Systems

- **Electronic Funds Transfer (EFT)**: Electronic transfer of money from one bank account to another.
- **Credit / Debit Cards**: Online card processing using 3D Secure verification.
- **Payment Gateways**: Third-party service that authorizes credit card/direct payments for e-businesses (e.g., PayPal, Stripe, PayHere).
- **Mobile Wallets**: Contactless digital wallet payment apps (e.g., Google Pay, Apple Pay).

---
## 4. Enterprise Information Systems

- **ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning)**: Integrated software system that manages all core business operations across departments (Finance, HR, Manufacturing, Sales, Inventory).
- **SCM (Supply Chain Management)**: Tracks raw materials, inventory, and finished products from original supplier to final consumer.
- **CRM (Customer Relationship Management)**: Manages customer interactions, leads, sales history, and support tickets to boost customer retention.
- **KMS (Knowledge Management System)**: Captures and shares corporate knowledge, procedures, and expertise within an enterprise.

---
## 5. Modern Work Concepts

- **Virtual Organization**: A network of independent companies/freelancers linked by ICT to share skills and costs without physical headquarters.
- **Teleworking / Remote Work**: Performing work duties outside traditional office locations using ICT communication tools.
